Republic of Koyash/Kingdom of Tiamat

The death of empires leaves chaos in their wake. The downfall of the Megiddoin Empire was no different

Foolish overseas military adventure lead to the collapse of the government and a decade long civil war

The international community stepped in and carved the dying empire into new countries following geographic lines. 

It falls to you, the new government, to lead your country. But will you achieve greatness or repeat the mistakes of your ancestors?

Republic of Koyash/Kingdom of Tiamat

Both countries are

Economically poor

Low levels of female empowerment

Outdated educational facilities

Lack of democratic principals

Highly discriminatory laws based on ethnic and religious identity. 

Your Neighbours 

Linshia Union

Atheistic autocracy, centered around  a strong leader and cabinet that controls most of the state. 

While controlled at home they offer military and economic assistance to other countries with no additional upfront requirements

Thearchy of Shantia

Part of the Maastricht Khaganate – a group of states that have agreed to sacrifice some of the autonomy in order to cooperate on common economic and security goals

Secular and progressive

Offer significant economic and social aid, but in exchange for systematic reforms to align your politics with theirs

Donfia Kingdoms 

A collection of tribal kingdoms, mostly secular and moving towards The Maastricht Khaganate for economic growth and cultural standards

Represents multiple ethnic identities that became independent when the Megiddoin Empire collapsed

The games

There will be a series of events that happen to both countries, you as a group will need to decide the direction of your country.

The games are split into 2 phases

These start with a blind bidding game

This is followed by a series of events that effect the future of your country. 

The outcomes of these events will depend on your decisions for the blind bidding game at the beginning. Of the phase

There is 1 additional choice of international partners. The choice in phase 1 will have an impact on political points for phase 2 

Blind Bidding

You start with 15 points of political power. 

This power can be spent in the following categories:

Economy

Military

Security

Welfare

Education

Each category can have a maximum of 10 power spent

You will need to decide as a group how to spend that power

Some decisions will be locked out by the amount you spend on categories

If any category drops below 0 you lose

Events

Event 1 – Linshia Union has attacked our neighbouring Donfia Kingdom, Refugees are heading towards our boarders, what do you do?

Place the military on the boarder (military score >= 6 points) This forces refugees to go to the other country (Koyash/Tiamat)

Allow all the refugees into your country (large impacts on welfare, education, economy, and security) – welfare spending will influence the result.

Negotiate with other country (Koyash/Tiamat) to take half the refugees each (lower impact on welfare, education, economy, and security)

Negotiate with other country (Koyash/Tiamat) for them to take all the refugees in exchange for some economic support
