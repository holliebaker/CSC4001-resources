Republic of Koyash/Kingdom of Tiamat

Both countries…

  • are economically poor

  • have Low levels of female empowerment

  • have Outdated educational facilities

  • Lack democratic principals

  • have Highly discriminatory laws based on ethnic and religious identity. 

Your Neighbours: Linshia Union

  • Atheistic autocracy, centered around  a strong leader and cabinet that controls most of the state. 

  • While controlled at home they offer military and economic assistance to other countries with no additional upfront requirements

Your neighbours: Thearchy of Shantia

  • Part of the Maastricht Khaganate – a group of states that have agreed to sacrifice some of the autonomy in order to cooperate on common economic and security goals

  • Secular and progressive

  • Offer significant economic and social aid, but in exchange for systematic reforms to align your politics with theirs

Your neighbours: Donfia Kingdoms 

  • A collection of tribal kingdoms, mostly secular and moving towards The Maastricht Khaganate for economic growth and cultural standards

  • Represents multiple ethnic identities that became independent when the Megiddoin Empire collapsed

The games

  • There will be a series of events that happen to both countries. You (as a team) will need to decide the direction of your country.

  • The games are split into 2 phases

    • Start with a blind bidding game

    • Folowed by a series of events that effect the future of your country. 

  • e outcomes of these events will depend on your decisions for the blind bidding game at the beginning. Of the phase

  • One al choice of international partners. The choice in phase 1 will have an impact on political points for phase 2 

nd Bidding

You start with 15 points of political power. 

This power can be spent in the following categories:

  • Economy

  • Military

  • Security

  • Welfare

  • Education

Each category can have a maximum of 10 power spent

You decide how to spend that power

Some decisions will be locked out by the amount you spend on categories

If any category drops below 0 you lose

Events

Event 1

Linshia Union has attacked our neighbouring Donfia Kingdom, Refugees are heading towards our boarders, what do you do?

  1. Place the military on the boarder (military score >= 6 points) This forces refugees to go to the other country (Koyash/Tiamat)

  2. Allow all the refugees into your country (large impacts on welfare, education, economy, and security) – welfare spending will influence the result.

  3. Negotiate with other country (Koyash/Tiamat) to take half the refugees each (lower impact on welfare, education, economy, and security)

  4. Negotiate with other country (Koyash/Tiamat) for them to take all the refugees in exchange for some economic support
